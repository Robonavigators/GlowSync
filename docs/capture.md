# Screen capture and lifecycle

GlowSync requests Android MediaProjection consent and starts a media-projection foreground service. On supported Android versions it requests capture of the default display. An active session may continue after the activity leaves the foreground while Android and the TV permit it. The user can stop from the app or foreground notification; sleep and projection revocation end the session. A fresh system prompt is needed for a new session, including after process death.

The service acquires the latest ImageReader image, samples a low-resolution image and closes it. It does not record video, save screenshots, or upload frames. Android may block DRM-protected media and secure windows. External HDMI video may be displayed through a TV-specific pipeline that MediaProjection cannot capture, even if the streaming app runs on an Apple TV, NVIDIA Shield or another external device. An HDMI box is therefore not a proven DRM workaround. A dark frame cannot reliably identify why capture is black; optional dark-frame behavior also affects genuinely dark scenes.

**External HDMI compatibility check (not yet validated on physical hardware):** Start with an unprotected, high-contrast changing colour pattern on the external device, grant normal MediaProjection consent on the TV, and compare GlowSync's on-screen preview against the HDMI picture. If the preview stays black or shows a different Android surface, do not assume DRM playback will work. Record the TV/Android version, HDMI source and observed result without publishing private captured frames. Do not attempt to bypass secure-content restrictions.

The service handles supported captured-content resize callbacks using the existing virtual display. Firmware behavior, revocation, sleep, rotation and background operation still need verification on each physical TV. See [privacy.md](privacy.md) and [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
